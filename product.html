The Ritual

Anoint. Stimulate. Illuminate.

A quiet sequence. Three to five drops, warmed between the palms. Pressed into the scalp. Drawn through the lengths.

01
Anoint

Warm the elixir between the palms. Press into the mid-lengths and ends, or apply section by section for the evening ritual.

02
Stimulate

Work slowly into the scalp with fingertips. Let the movement awaken circulation and prepare the fiber to receive.

03
Illuminate

Leave it to settle. Leave it to disappear. What remains is softness, polish, and light.
